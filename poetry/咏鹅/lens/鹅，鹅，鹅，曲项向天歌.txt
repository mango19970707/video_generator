风格：二次元动漫，中式古风，水墨画。
**Low Angle: A solitary white goose stands at the edge of a mist-lit pond at dawn, its arched neck silhouetted against a pale gold sky. Delicate ripples spread beneath its feet, distorting the reflection of soft clouds. The bird’s pure white plumage glows with a subtle iridescence, contrasting against washes of cool gray and blush pink in the water. Above, faint brushstroke-like wisps of vapor curl like ancient calligraphy across the sky. The composition embraces vast negative space—empty sky and still water—emphasizing solitude and grace. A single feather drifts downward in slow motion, catching the light, as if the poem itself is breathing. Color tone: ethereal pastels—pearl white, dawn pink, silver mist—with ink-wash depth at the frame’s edge. Silence hums beneath the image, as though sound has been transmuted into form.**
