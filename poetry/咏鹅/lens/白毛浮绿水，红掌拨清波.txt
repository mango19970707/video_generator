风格：二次元动漫，中式古风，水墨画。
**Lens Angle: Wide-angle, slightly low elevation, capturing the surface of a mist-veiled pond at dawn.**  

A lone white goose floats serenely on glassy jade-green water, its pure white feathers softly blurred by rising morning haze. The stillness is broken only by gentle ripples—delicate crimson webbed feet part the clear, mirror-like waves beneath, sending concentric rings shimmering outward in silence. Above, a faint blush of peach lingers in the sky, echoing the red of the goose’s feet, while the surrounding reeds fade into soft focus, leaving vast negative space that emphasizes solitude and grace. The composition embraces traditional shanshui balance—water below, sky above, subject centered yet floating in emptiness. Light filters diagonally from upper left, gilding the goose’s silhouette with a subtle halo. Color palette: muted celadon, ivory, pale coral, and ink-wash gray. A single drifting lotus leaf drifts mid-distance, motionless except for the ripple it barely touches. Ethereal. Tranquil. Poised between dream and reality.
