风格：二次元动漫，中式古风，水墨画。
**Lens Angle: Wide-angle, slightly low elevation, capturing reflection and sky.**  
Dawn light washes a still pond in soft gold and pale jade, mist curling like brushstrokes above the water. A single white goose glides across the frame, its form mirrored perfectly beneath, breaking the surface with a whisper of ripples that fade into silence. The neck curves in an elegant arc—ink on silk—its orange beak tipped with morning light. Reeds at the margins blur into negative space, leaving two-thirds of the frame open to water and sky, embodying classical balance. Above, a faint blush of peach lingers on the horizon, echoing the gentle flush of lotus buds just emerging near the bank. The color tone is muted: celadon, ivory, and haze-blue, with subtle gradients suggesting breath rather than motion. One feather drifts downward, caught mid-air in slow descent—a moment suspended, as if the poem itself hesitates.
